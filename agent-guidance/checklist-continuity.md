## Continuation across compaction

At each completed checklist item and material decision, refresh the task's
checkpoint with the exact approved checklist, checkbox states, approval evidence,
decisions and rationale, and targeted recovery reading for the next item. Follow
the context-continuity skill when available; otherwise preserve those facts in
the existing handoff format.

After compaction or resume, recover the next item's relevant ontology, ownership
and existing implementation before editing. Reconcile partial operations and
current diffs. Continue from the first unfinished item under the existing
approval; compaction itself does not require another approval, full audit, or
checklist rewrite. Ask only when new evidence materially invalidates the approved
plan or leaves consequential intent unresolved.
