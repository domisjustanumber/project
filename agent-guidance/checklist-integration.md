## Coordinating multiple workstreams

When acting as the overview/integration coordinator for several owner agents or
chats, use the companion `multi-workstream-integration` skill. Each owner still uses
this linear checklist workflow; the coordinator maintains the dependency/approval
overview and gives compact linked reports rather than reprinting every owner's
checklist at every milestone. Relay direct human instructions verbatim, separately
from coordinator interpretation. The companion skill defines the coordination cycle.
